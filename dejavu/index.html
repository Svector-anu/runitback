  • Stocks and tokens Follow a ticker or a token address across the markets it touches.

Give it a trail. Watch it work.

OpenCrawl follows one thread through the market and keeps the evidence it finds along the way.

  1. Start with a subject

    Enter a stock ticker, a token address, or a wallet.

  2. Investigate the activity

    The agent looks at market activity and the on-chain relationships around it.

  3. Watch the spider move

    Every action shows on the page: where it looks, what it opens, what it finds.

  4. Inspect the evidence

    Each connection points back to the source it came from.

  5. Open the terminal

    Charts, wallet relationships, and the agent's evidence in one investigation view.

What works today

OpenCrawl is an early build. This is what is real, what is placeholder, and what is not connected yet.

Working now

  • The spider: movement, silk, and the event interface that drives it.
  • The investigation terminal layout.
  • A simulated investigation that sends the same events a live agent would.

Demonstration only

  • Findings, connections, and evidence from simulated runs. They are labeled as demonstration data.

Not connected yet

  • Market data and charts.
  • On-chain and wallet data.
  • The computer-use agent.
